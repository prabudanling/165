# 1. PURPOSE

Dokumen ini menetapkan arsitektur tata kelola dan **decision rights** untuk 165.

Tujuannya adalah memastikan bahwa:

- keputusan dibuat oleh pihak yang tepat,
- tanggung jawab tidak tumpang tindih,
- kewenangan founder jelas,
- kepentingan institusi terlindungi,
- kualitas ilmiah dan editorial terlindungi,
- aset dan arsip terlindungi,
- keputusan dapat diaudit,
- konflik dapat diselesaikan,
- organisasi dapat tumbuh tanpa kehilangan arah,
- dan 165 dapat tetap berjalan lintas generasi.

Dokumen ini merupakan **governance framework internal**. Ia tidak dengan sendirinya menggantikan anggaran dasar, badan hukum, kontrak kerja, peraturan negara, atau dokumen legal lain yang berlaku.

---

# 2. GOVERNANCE PHILOSOPHY

165 dibangun dengan prinsip:

> **Clear authority, accountable power, documented decisions, protected mission.**

Empat asas utamanya:

## 2.1 Mission Protection

Visi dan misi inti 165 tidak boleh berubah hanya karena pergantian orang, tren teknologi, tekanan komersial, atau kepentingan jangka pendek.

## 2.2 Authority With Accountability

Kewenangan selalu disertai kewajiban untuk menjelaskan alasan keputusan dan dampaknya.

## 2.3 Domain Expertise

Keputusan teknis harus ditentukan oleh orang yang memiliki kompetensi relevan.

Contoh:

- keputusan scholarly → research authority,
- keputusan archive → archive authority,
- keputusan security → security authority,
- keputusan finance → finance authority.

## 2.4 Separation of Roles

Tidak semua keputusan boleh diambil oleh orang yang sama.

165 harus menghindari:

> one person = creator + reviewer + approver + financial controller

untuk keputusan yang berdampak tinggi.

# 5. GOVERNANCE BODIES

## 5.1 Founder

Founder adalah penjaga visi, identitas, dan kontinuitas strategis 165.

Founder tidak harus menjadi pengambil seluruh keputusan operasional sehari-hari.

Peran founder adalah terutama:

- menjaga mission,
- menjaga identity,
- menjaga constitutional principles,
- menetapkan strategic direction,
- melindungi founder-created intellectual property sesuai aturan yang berlaku,
- mengangkat atau menyetujui pejabat strategis,
- menjaga continuity,
- dan memastikan 165 tidak keluar dari mandat dasarnya.

---

# 6. FOUNDER'S RESERVED POWERS

Founder memiliki reserved powers pada keputusan yang menyentuh identitas dan keberlangsungan fundamental 165.

Reserved powers yang disarankan:

### A. Identity

Persetujuan terhadap perubahan:

- official name,
- core mission,
- core vision,
- foundational brand,
- constitutional purpose.

### B. Mission

Persetujuan terhadap perubahan besar yang menggeser 165 dari knowledge/heritage/preservation platform menjadi model yang secara substansial berbeda.

### C. Structural Change

Persetujuan terhadap:

- merger,
- dissolution,
- sale of core institutional assets,
- transfer of controlling rights,
- fundamental governance restructuring.

### D. Core IP

Persetujuan atas pelepasan atau transfer aset IP inti yang secara material menentukan identitas 165, subject to applicable law and formal legal instruments.

### E. Succession Framework

Founder berwenang mengusulkan atau menetapkan mekanisme succession sebagaimana diatur dalam Founder Charter dan dokumen konstitusional.

### F. Mission Safeguard

Founder dapat meminta review khusus apabila keputusan organisasi dinilai berpotensi mengubah mission secara fundamental.

**Catatan:** reserved powers harus diterjemahkan ke instrumen legal yang sesuai apabila 165 menjadi badan hukum atau struktur korporasi formal.

---

# 7. LIMITS OF FOUNDER AUTHORITY

Founder authority bukan berarti founder boleh melakukan seluruh hal tanpa kontrol.

Untuk keputusan tertentu, founder harus tetap tunduk pada:

- hukum yang berlaku,
- contractual obligations,
- fiduciary duties apabila berlaku,
- rights of donors/contributors,
- privacy obligations,
- intellectual-property law,
- archival rights,
- scholarly integrity,
- documented governance procedures.

Founder tidak boleh mengubah fakta sejarah hanya karena kepentingan branding.

Founder juga tidak boleh memperlakukan otoritas spiritual TQN sebagai aset komersial pribadi 165.

# 12. DECISION AUTHORITY LEVELS

## LEVEL 0 — INDIVIDUAL / ROUTINE

Contoh:

- penjadwalan post,
- typo correction,
- resizing visual,
- routine admin.

Dapat didelegasikan.

## LEVEL 1 — DOMAIN DECISION

Contoh:

- editorial format,
- technical implementation,
- archive metadata,
- research workflow.

Diputuskan oleh domain lead.

## LEVEL 2 — CROSS-FUNCTIONAL

Contoh:

- perubahan taxonomy,
- perubahan website IA,
- launch membership,
- major content campaign.

Memerlukan cross-functional review.

## LEVEL 3 — STRATEGIC

Contoh:

- major partnership,
- major revenue model,
- significant technology migration,
- international expansion.

Memerlukan executive + appropriate governing approval.

## LEVEL 4 — RESERVED / CONSTITUTIONAL

Contoh:

- mission change,
- dissolution,
- transfer of core IP,
- major structural transformation.

Founder + governing mechanism sesuai constitutional framework.

# 14. RACI STANDARD

For major projects use:

- **R — Responsible**
- **A — Accountable**
- **C — Consulted**
- **I — Informed**

Rule:

> Every significant initiative must have exactly one accountable decision owner unless the governing document explicitly requires joint authority.

---

# 15. CONFLICT OF INTEREST

Every person with material decision authority should disclose relevant conflicts.

Examples:

- vendor relationship,
- financial interest,
- family interest,
- publication conflict,
- institutional competition,
- undisclosed sponsorship.

A conflicted person may be:

- consulted for expertise,
- but excluded from approval,
- or required to abstain,

depending on severity.

---

# 16. EDITORIAL INDEPENDENCE

Commercial interests must not dictate historical conclusions.

For example:

A sponsor may fund:

> digitization of an archive

but may not dictate:

> what the archive must conclude historically.

Sponsored content must be clearly distinguishable from editorial research.

---

# 17. SCHOLARLY REVIEW

For historically sensitive claims:

```text
AUTHOR
  ↓
SOURCE CHECK
  ↓
RESEARCH REVIEW
  ↓
EDITORIAL REVIEW
  ↓
PUBLICATION
```

For highly controversial claims:

```text
AUTHOR
  ↓
PRIMARY SOURCE REVIEW
  ↓
SPECIALIST REVIEW
  ↓
EDITORIAL REVIEW
  ↓
PUBLISH WITH EVIDENCE STATUS
```

The system should preserve uncertainty instead of forcing false certainty.

---

# 18. ARCHIVE DECISION RULE

The authority to publish a digital object is not identical to ownership of the underlying object.

Before publication:

1. verify provenance,
2. determine rights,
3. determine consent,
4. classify sensitivity,
5. assign metadata,
6. determine access level,
7. preserve master copy,
8. create publication derivative.

---

# 19. FINANCIAL GOVERNANCE

Financial powers must be separated.

Recommended controls:

### Founder/Board
Strategic budget approval.

### Finance Lead
Accounting and reporting.

### Operations
Execution within approved budget.

### Independent review
Periodic review/audit appropriate to organization size and legal structure.

High-value transactions should require dual authorization.

---

# 20. PROCUREMENT

Vendor selection should consider:

- capability,
- security,
- cost,
- sustainability,
- interoperability,
- contractual rights,
- exit strategy.

Do not select technology vendors solely because they are cheap.

Critical vendors require:

- data export capability,
- backup,
- contract review,
- termination plan,
- ownership clarity.

---

# 21. DELEGATION

Founder and executives should delegate authority using written scopes.

Every delegation defines:

- authority,
- limits,
- budget,
- duration,
- reporting,
- revocation condition.

Delegation does not automatically transfer ultimate accountability.

---

# 22. EMERGENCY AUTHORITY

Emergency decision-making may be accelerated when delay could cause:

- security breach,
- data loss,
- archive destruction,
- major legal exposure,
- financial fraud,
- severe service outage.

Emergency decisions must be:

1. documented,
2. limited to the emergency,
3. reviewed afterward,
4. added to the decision log.

Emergency authority must not become a permanent bypass around governance.

---

# 23. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Disputes should progress through:

### Stage 1 — Direct clarification
Resolve factual misunderstanding.

### Stage 2 — Domain review
Relevant expert reviews the issue.

### Stage 3 — Executive review
Operational disagreement is escalated.

### Stage 4 — Governing review
Major institutional disputes.

### Stage 5 — Constitutional / legal process
Fundamental disputes.

For historical disputes, the preferred outcome is often:

> document the evidence and competing interpretations

rather than force a premature single narrative.

---

# 24. RECONSIDERATION & APPEAL

A decision may be reconsidered when:

- new evidence appears,
- material error is demonstrated,
- conflict of interest is discovered,
- applicable law changes,
- a material risk was omitted,
- decision authority was exceeded.

Reconsideration does not guarantee reversal.

---

# 25. TRANSPARENCY

165 should maintain appropriate public transparency around:

- governance structure,
- editorial standards,
- correction policy,
- sponsorship principles,
- major institutional partnerships,
- archive rights,
- privacy principles,
- major changes.

Not every internal decision must be public.

Transparency must be balanced against:

- privacy,
- security,
- contractual confidentiality,
- contributor safety,
- archive restrictions.

---

# 26. AI GOVERNANCE

AI is a tool, not an independent authority.

AI-generated material involving history or TQN must be subject to human review appropriate to risk.

AI must not be used as the sole source for:

- genealogy claims,
- spiritual authority,
- historical attribution,
- manuscript authenticity,
- biographies,
- allegations about people.

For high-risk material:

> **Human source verification is mandatory.**

---

# 27. PUBLIC REPRESENTATION

Only designated representatives may issue statements representing 165 institutionally.

Social media administrators must not automatically have authority to:

- speak for the institution,
- announce governance decisions,
- make spiritual claims,
- announce partnerships,
- make legal commitments.

Official communications should have a verifiable approval path.

---

# 28. SPIRITUAL AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

165 is an information, heritage, research, education, media, community, and preservation platform.

It must not manufacture or simulate spiritual authority.

165 must not imply:

- digital bai'at,
- digital talqin,
- online appointment of mursyid,
- spiritual ranking through website membership,
- invented ijazah,
- invented silsilah,
- fabricated miracles,
- fabricated quotations.

Where religious or spiritual teaching is presented, the provenance and nature of the source must be clear.

---

# 29. MEMBERSHIP AUTHORITY

Digital membership grants access to digital platform functions.

It does not confer:

- spiritual status,
- religious rank,
- institutional office,
- authority to represent a TQN institution,
- authorization to teach on behalf of a TQN authority.

Any institutional role must be granted through a valid institutional process.

---

# 30. SUCCESSION

165 must not depend indefinitely on one individual.

Succession planning should identify:

- mission custodians,
- executive successors,
- archive successors,
- technical successors,
- credential holders,
- domain administrators,
- legal signatories,
- emergency contacts.

There must be a recoverable record of:

- domains,
- repositories,
- credentials,
- vendor accounts,
- legal documents,
- backups,
- archive masters,
- licenses,
- financial records.

Sensitive credentials must **never** be placed directly inside public repository documents.

- institutional memory,

# 35. DECISION QUALITY CHECK

Before approving a significant decision, ask:

### Mission
Does it protect 165's mission?

### Evidence
Do we have sufficient evidence?

### Authority
Is the right person approving?

### Risk
What can go wrong?

### Reversibility
Can we undo it?

### Cost
What is the financial/operational cost?

### Security
Does it create new security exposure?

### Legal
Does it create IP/privacy/contract risk?

### Archive
Does it affect preservation?

### Future
Will this decision remain sensible at larger scale?

---

# 36. FOUR-KEY GOVERNANCE TEST

No major decision should pass without answering:

> **Who decides?**  
> **Based on what evidence?**  
> **Who reviews it?**  
> **Where is it recorded?**

# 40. GOVERNANCE TRANSITION TRIGGER

165 should consider formalizing the next governance layer when any of the following occurs:

- team exceeds practical founder-only management capacity,
- multiple institutional partnerships exist,
- recurring revenue becomes material,
- significant donations/grants are received,
- third-party archives are entrusted to 165,
- significant personal data is processed,
- multiple countries are involved,
- research reputation becomes material,
- legal entity structure is established,
- operational risk becomes material.

Governance should evolve based on actual complexity, not prestige.
