# 🇮🇩 Bahasa Indonesia

> **Kami tidak mengarang sejarah. Kami menyimpannya — dengan sumber, dengan bukti, dengan kejujuran.**
> 165 adalah platform warisan digital untuk Thariqat Qodiriyah wa Naqsyabandiyah (TQN): katalog tokoh, tempat, naskah, dokumen, dan **rantai sanad** yang bersumber — dirancang agar dapat diwariskan utuh ke anak-cucu, bukan sekadar dipajang.

## 🕌 Mengapa 165 Ada?

Setiap majlis thariqat menyimpan pengetahuan yang hidup di ingatan manusia: silsilah guru, riwayat pesantren, tulisan para mursyid, tradisi yang diwariskan lisan. Ketika para penjaga itu pulang kepada Allah, **pengetahuan yang tidak dicatat bisa hilang dalam satu generasi.**

165 lahir untuk menjawab satu pertanyaan sederhana namun berat:

> *Bagaimana menyimpan warisan thariqat secara digital — tanpa menambahkan satu pun nama yang tidak bersumber?*

Jawabannya bukan sekadar "website". Jawabannya adalah **sistem disiplin**: setiap data punya sumber, setiap sumber punya tingkat kepercayaan, setiap perubahan tercatat, dan setiap kekosongan **dinyatakan apa adanya** — bukan diisi karangan.

## 📜 Doktrin: Enam Pilar 165

Ini bukan sekadar aturan teknis — ini adalah **janji kepada yang akan datang membaca kita seratus tahun dari sekarang.**

### 1. Sumber Utama (Source-First)
Tidak ada data yang masuk tanpa sumber yang disebut namanya. Bila sumber tak ada, datanya tak masuk — dan kekosongan itu *tampil*, bukan disembunyikan.

### 2. Bukti Bergraduasi A–F
Setiap informasi membawa peringkat kepercayaan yang tampil di antarmuka:

| Tingkat | Arti | Contoh |
|:---:|---|---|
| **A** | **Sumber Primer** — manuskrip, dokumen, arsip dalam simpanan 165 | Naskah ijazah, kitab silsilah |
| **B** | **Sumber Institusional Otoritatif** — catatan yang diterbitkan 165 sendiri | Registri internal, kartu profil |
| **C** | **Sumber Keilmuan** — literatur akademik terbitan, dengan sitasi | Jurnal, buku sejarah |
| **D** | **Sejarah Lisan** — riwayat tradisional, disimpan sebagai kesaksian, **tidak pernah** sebagai fakta | Tutur para abah |
| **E** | **Catatan Komunitas** — kiriman publik, menunggu tinjauan editorial | Kiriman jamaah via Kontribusi |
| **F** | **Tidak Terverifikasi** — ditampilkan dengan ketidakpastian eksplisit, tak pernah dipromosikan diam-diam | Klaim belum ditemukan sumbernya |

### 3. Murni-Tambah (Pure Append)
Sejarah tidak boleh "diedit rapi". Revisi membuat versi baru; versi lama tetap ada. Bahkan kiriman publik **tidak pernah dihapus** oleh proses teknis apa pun — sesuatu yang pernah kami selamatkan lewat *doctrine* khusus dalam seeder.

### 4. ID Global
Setiap entitas punya identitas permanen lintas-sistem: `165-PERSON-000009`, `165-SANAD-000003`, `165-COLL-000002`. Ketika data berpindah ke sistem lain, identitasnya tetap bisa dilacak.

### 5. Kejujuran Sumber-Ganda
Bila dua sumber berselisih, **keduanya ditampilkan berdampingan** dengan penanda `DISPUTED` — bukan disenyapkan, bukan diadu tanpa konteks.

### 6. Keamanan Sanad — *Tidak Pernah Mengarang Nama*
> Inilah pilar yang paling kami jaga.

Sanad adalah amanah. Untuk rantai silsilah TQN se-dunia, 165 berlaku tegas: **nama hanya boleh masuk bila dinamai oleh sumber.** Segmen silsilah yang sumbernya belum terdokumentasi ditampilkan sebagai **MATA BLOK** (tanda peringatan kuning) — segmen yang diakui ada, namun *menunggu transkripsi kitab silsilah* — dengan keterangan jujur, bukan diisi nama karangan.

Hasilnya: rantai yang terisi itu **layak diwartakan**, dan celah yang tersisa itu **sengaja ditampakkan** — karena di sanalah letak kerja rumah selanjutnya: dari jamaah, keluarga, dan arsip yang dikirim melalui kanal Kontribusi.

### 🛡️ Penemuan Kunci: Ketahanan Serverless

Pernahkah sebuah situs "kehilangan datanya" setelah dipindahkan ke Vercel — angka jadi `0`, sanad lenyap? **Itu pernah terjadi di 165.** Penyebabnya sederhana dan jujur: SQLite adalah berkas lokal, sedangkan serverless (Vercel) tidak memuat berkas itu.

Solusinya menjadi salah satu fitur paling berharga di 165:

1. `bun run db:export` mengekspor seluruh basis data ke `src/data/heritage-snapshot.json`
2. Snapshot itu **ter-bundle ke dalam aplikasi** saat build
3. `queries.ts` mencoba basis data dulu; bila gagal (serverless), ia **meneruskan permintaan ke snapshot secara transparan** — tanpa satupun komponen yang tahu bedanya
4. Hasilnya: angka, sanad, dan dokumen **selalu tampil di mana pun 165 dipasang** — lokal, Vercel, maupun berkas statis

> Pelajaran kami: *keindahan tanpa ketahanan adalah hiasan. Ketahanan tanpa kejujuran adalah tipuan. 165 memilih keduanya.*

## 🌐 178 Bahasa, Satu Warisan

- **Bahasa Indonesia** adalah bawaan resmi — sejak detik pertama server merender (SSR), bukan kedip-kedip setelah halaman terbuka
- **6 bahasa kurasi tangan penuh**: Indonesia, Inggris, Melayu, Jawa, Sunda, Arab — ditulis menghormati register lokal masing-masing
- **Target seluruh 172 bahasa kurasi AI** (semua bahasa non-kurasi di registry — amanat "seluruh bahasa, tak satu pun terlewat") lewat pipeline terjemahan institusional: placeholder terkunci, brand terkunci, register hormat, skrip asli; kamus masuk progresif (resume-safe), bahasa yang kamusnya belum siap menampilkan Bahasa Indonesia
- **11 bahasa penuh RTL**: Arab, Farsy, Urdu, Ibrani, Pashto, Sindhi, Uighur, Kurdi, Yiddish, Divehi, Sorani — `dir="rtl"` benar-benar menyala
- **Pemilih bahasa yang jujur**: titik hijau = kurasi, titik kuning = AI, dan bahasa yang kamusnya belum siap **menampilkan Indonesia — bukan kosong, bukan angka aneh**
- **Konten warisan tetap verbatim**: antarmuka berbahasa apa pun, isi dokumen dan sanad tetap dalam bahasa sumbernya — *karena menerjemahkan naskah adalah tugas ulama, bukan mesin*

# 🇬🇧 English

> **We do not invent history. We preserve it — with sources, with evidence, with honesty.**
> 165 is a digital heritage platform for the Qodiriyah wa Naqsyabandiyah Tariqa (TQN): a catalog of figures, places, manuscripts, documents, and **sourced spiritual genealogy (sanad)** — engineered to be inherited intact by generations to come, not merely displayed.

---

## 🕌 Why 165 Exists

Every tariqa majlis carries knowledge that lives in human memory: the lineage of its teachers, the history of its schools, the writings of its murshids, traditions passed down orally. When its guardians return to Allah, **knowledge that was never written down can vanish within a single generation.**

165 was born to answer one simple yet weighty question:

> *How do you digitally preserve a tariqa's heritage — without adding a single name that has no source?*

The answer is not just "a website." The answer is a **discipline system**: every datum has a source, every source has a confidence grade, every change is recorded, and every gap is **declared as it is** — never filled with invention.

## 📜 The Doctrine: Six Pillars of 165

These are not mere technical rules — they are a **promise to whoever will read us a hundred years from now.**

### 1. Source-First
No datum enters without a named source. If the source is absent, the datum does not enter — and that absence is *displayed*, not hidden.

### 2. Graded Evidence A–F
Every piece of information carries a confidence grade, shown right in the interface:

| Grade | Meaning | Example |
|:---:|---|---|
| **A** | **Primary Source** — manuscripts, documents, archival records held by 165 | Initiation certificates, genealogy books |
| **B** | **Authoritative Institutional Source** — records issued by 165 itself | Internal registry, profile cards |
| **C** | **Scholarly Source** — published academic literature, cited | Journals, history books |
| **D** | **Oral History** — traditional accounts, preserved as testimony, **never** as fact | Narratives of the elders |
| **E** | **Community Record** — public submissions, pending editorial review | Congregant submissions via Contribute |
| **F** | **Unverified** — displayed with explicit uncertainty, never silently promoted | Claims without yet-found sources |

### 3. Pure Append
History must not be "tidied by editing". Revisions create new versions; old versions remain. Even public submissions are **never deleted** by any technical process — something we explicitly protected through a dedicated *doctrine* in the seeder.

### 4. Global IDs
Every entity carries a permanent cross-system identity: `165-PERSON-000009`, `165-SANAD-000003`, `165-COLL-000002`. When data moves to another system, its identity remains traceable.

### 5. Multi-Source Honesty
When two sources disagree, **both are shown side by side** with a `DISPUTED` marker — never silenced, never thrown into battle without context.

### 6. Sanad Safety — *Names Are Never Invented*
> This is the pillar we guard most fiercely.

A sanad is a trust. For the global TQN lineage, 165 is strict: **a name may only enter when a source names it.** Lineage segments whose sources are not yet documented are shown as **BLOCKED LINKS** (amber warning marks) — segments acknowledged to exist but *awaiting transcription of the genealogy books* — labeled honestly, never filled with invented names.

The result: the chains that are filled are **worth proclaiming**, and the gaps that remain are **intentionally visible** — because that is exactly where the next work lies: from congregants, families, and archives submitted through the Contribution channel.

### 🛡️ Key Insight: Serverless Resilience

Has a website ever "lost its data" after moving to Vercel — counters drop to `0`, the sanad disappears? **It happened to 165.** The cause is simple and honest: SQLite is a local file, and serverless (Vercel) does not ship that file.

The solution became one of 165's most valuable features:

1. `bun run db:export` exports the entire database to `src/data/heritage-snapshot.json`
2. That snapshot is **bundled into the application** at build time
3. `queries.ts` tries the database first; if unavailable (serverless), it **transparently falls through to the snapshot** — not a single component knows the difference
4. The result: numbers, sanad, and documents **always display wherever 165 is installed** — locally, on Vercel, or as a static site

> Our lesson: *beauty without resilience is decoration. Resilience without honesty is deception. 165 chooses both.*

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Does 165 claim absolute truth?</b></summary>

No. Every piece of information carries an A–F evidence grade and a verification status, displayed honestly. When sources disagree, both are shown side by side. Only the source is absolute — never the interpretation.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why are some sanad segments "empty"?</b></summary>

That is not negligence — that is a **BLOCKED LINK**: a segment acknowledged to exist by genealogy-book convention, but whose per-name sources are not yet documented in 165. We do not invent names to close gaps. The gap is, in fact, an *invitation*: send the source, and it will be filled honorably.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why isn't document content translated into 178 languages?</b></summary>

The interface is translated; **heritage content remains verbatim**. Translating scripture, certificates, and genealogies demands scholarly and religious care — not machine labor. 165 chooses to honestly preserve the original rather than mistranslate.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>My site shows 0 / lost data after deployment?</b></summary>

Almost certainly the snapshot was not exported. Run `bun run db:export`, commit `heritage-snapshot.json`, redeploy. This is a scar we turned into a system.
</details>

<summary><b>Can other tariqa or organizations use the 165 pattern?</b></summary>

Please do. 165's doctrine, schema, and resilience patterns are designed to be generic for preserving any heritage — tariqas, boarding schools, families, even non-religious institutions that value sources and honesty. Fork with blessing. 💚
</details>

Decision rights are defined in the canon document **165 Governance and Decision Rights v1.0** (readable in the Documents Room). Public submissions belong to the community — no technical process has the right to delete them.
