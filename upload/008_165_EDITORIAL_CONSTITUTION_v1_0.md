# 1. PURPOSE

Dokumen ini merupakan konstitusi editorial 165.web.id.

Ia menetapkan standar mengenai bagaimana 165:

- meneliti,
- menilai sumber,
- menulis,
- mengutip,
- menerjemahkan,
- mengedit,
- memberi konteks,
- menerbitkan,
- memperbarui,
- mengoreksi,
- menarik kembali,
- mendokumentasikan perbedaan riwayat,
- menggunakan foto/audio/video,
- dan menggunakan AI.

Tujuan akhirnya adalah:

> **Membangun kepercayaan melalui editorial discipline, bukan melalui klaim otoritas.**

- masyarakat umum,
- santri,
- jamaah,
- generasi muda,
- pendidik,
- peneliti,
- sejarawan,
- ahli manuskrip,
- perpustakaan,
- arsip,
- universitas,

# 2. EDITORIAL MISSION

165 menerbitkan pengetahuan untuk:

1. melestarikan memori TQN,
2. menjelaskan sejarah secara bertanggung jawab,
3. membuka akses terhadap sumber,
4. membantu pembaca memahami konteks,
5. mendorong penelitian,
6. mendokumentasikan warisan,
7. menghubungkan generasi,
8. dan menyediakan pengetahuan yang dapat ditelusuri kembali ke sumbernya.

165 tidak mengejar kontroversi demi traffic.

---

# 3. EDITORIAL NORTH STAR

> **“Document deeply. Attribute honestly. Explain clearly. Preserve uncertainty. Correct openly.”**

Terjemahan kerja:

> **Dokumentasikan dengan mendalam. Atribusikan dengan jujur. Jelaskan dengan jelas. Pelihara ketidakpastian. Koreksi secara terbuka.**

---

# 4. EDITORIAL NON-NEGOTIABLES

1. Tidak mengarang fakta.
2. Tidak mengarang kutipan.
3. Tidak mengarang silsilah.
4. Tidak mengarang tanggal.
5. Tidak mengarang manuskrip.
6. Tidak mengarang sumber.
7. Tidak mengubah fakta karena kebutuhan branding.
8. Tidak menghilangkan ketidakpastian hanya agar narasi terlihat meyakinkan.
9. Tidak menyamarkan interpretasi sebagai fakta primer.
10. Tidak menggunakan AI sebagai pengganti verifikasi manusia pada klaim penting.
11. Tidak memalsukan atau mengedit media sejarah dengan cara yang mengubah makna tanpa keterangan.
12. Tidak mengeksploitasi kesakralan untuk manipulasi komersial.

# 6. EDITORIAL PRINCIPLE: SOURCE BEFORE NARRATIVE

Urutan kerja normal:

```text
QUESTION
   ↓
SOURCE DISCOVERY
   ↓
SOURCE ASSESSMENT
   ↓
EVIDENCE EXTRACTION
   ↓
DRAFT
   ↓
ATTRIBUTION
   ↓
REVIEW
   ↓
PUBLICATION
```

Bukan:

```text
NARRATIVE
   ↓
SEARCH FOR SUPPORTING SOURCE
```

Kecuali artikel tersebut secara eksplisit berupa:

- editorial,
- commentary,
- opinion,
- interpretation.

Dan jenis konten harus diberi label yang jelas.

# 7. EPISTEMIC LABEL SYSTEM

Setiap materi informasional yang relevan harus dapat ditempatkan dalam salah satu kelas berikut.

## E1 — DOCUMENTED FACT

Didukung oleh sumber primer atau bukti dokumenter kuat.

Bahasa yang digunakan dapat bersifat langsung apabila evidensinya cukup.

Contoh:

> “Dokumen X bertanggal Y mencatat...”

## E2 — SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION

Kesimpulan atau interpretasi dari penelitian akademik.

Gunakan atribusi:

> “Menurut penelitian A...”

> “Peneliti B menafsirkan...”

## E3 — INSTITUTIONAL ACCOUNT

Riwayat yang diberikan atau diterbitkan oleh institusi terkait.

Gunakan label yang sesuai:

> “Menurut riwayat resmi [institusi]...”

## E4 — ORAL / LIVING TRADITION

Kesaksian atau riwayat lisan.

Gunakan formula:

> “Dalam kesaksian yang dihimpun 165...”

atau:

> “Menurut tradisi lisan yang hidup di...”

## E5 — 165 EDITORIAL SYNTHESIS

Sintesis yang dibuat oleh 165.

Harus dibedakan secara jelas dari sumber asli.

# 8. CLAIM CONFIDENCE

Selain jenis evidensi, klaim dapat diberi status:

### CONFIRMED
Didukung oleh bukti yang memadai dan tidak ditemukan kontradiksi material.

### WELL-SUPPORTED
Didukung oleh beberapa sumber yang kredibel, tetapi masih mungkin ada keterbatasan.

### SOURCE-DEPENDENT
Diterima berdasarkan sumber atau tradisi tertentu dan harus disajikan dengan atribusi.

### DISPUTED
Ada perbedaan sumber atau interpretasi yang material.

### UNRESOLVED
Bukti belum cukup untuk menyimpulkan.

### UNVERIFIED
Klaim belum berhasil diverifikasi.

Klaim UNVERIFIED tidak boleh ditulis dengan nada kepastian.

---

# 9. SOURCE HIERARCHY

Tidak ada satu hierarki sumber yang berlaku identik untuk semua jenis pertanyaan.

Namun sebagai baseline:

### Primary Sources

- manuscripts,
- letters,
- dated institutional documents,
- contemporary publications,
- photographs with reliable provenance,
- recordings,
- archival records,
- inscriptions,
- original objects.

### Secondary Sources

- peer-reviewed scholarship,
- academic monographs,
- theses/dissertations,
- critical studies,
- reputable reference works.

### Institutional Sources

- official histories,
- official websites,
- institutional publications,
- institutional archives.

### Community / Oral Sources

- oral testimony,
- family archives,
- community records,
- eyewitness accounts,
- living tradition.

### General Web / Social Media

Dapat digunakan untuk discovery, leads, atau contextual material, tetapi tidak otomatis menjadi authoritative evidence.

# 10. SOURCE EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

Setiap sumber penting dinilai berdasarkan:

### Provenance
Dari mana sumber berasal?

### Authenticity
Apakah keasliannya dapat didukung?

### Date
Kapan dibuat/dipublikasikan?

### Authorship
Siapa pembuatnya?

### Proximity
Seberapa dekat sumber dengan peristiwa?

### Transmission
Bagaimana sumber sampai kepada kita?

### Editorial History
Apakah pernah diedit atau diterbitkan ulang?

### Corroboration
Apakah terdapat sumber independen yang mendukung?

### Bias / Position
Apakah sumber memiliki posisi atau kepentingan tertentu?

### Preservation State
Apakah ada kerusakan atau kehilangan konteks?

# 12. CITATION STANDARD

Setiap klaim material harus dapat ditelusuri ke sumber.

Gunakan citation yang:

- konsisten,
- dapat ditemukan,
- tidak menyesatkan,
- membedakan primary dan secondary source,
- menjaga bibliographic completeness.

Untuk digital material, simpan sedapat mungkin:

- canonical URL,
- access date,
- archive/repository reference,
- persistent identifier jika tersedia.

---

# 13. DIRECT QUOTATION RULE

Kutipan langsung harus:

- benar-benar berasal dari sumber,
- tidak dipalsukan,
- tidak dipotong sehingga mengubah makna,
- mempertahankan ejaan asli jika relevan,
- diberi konteks.

Jika translasi dibuat oleh 165:

> tampilkan sebagai terjemahan dan, bila penting, simpan teks asli.

---

# 14. PARAPHRASE RULE

Parafrasa tetap membutuhkan attribution bila informasi berasal dari sumber tertentu.

Jangan mengubah:

> “peneliti menyatakan”

menjadi:

> “fakta menunjukkan”

hanya untuk membuat tulisan terdengar lebih kuat.

---

# 15. HISTORY WRITING STANDARD

Artikel sejarah 165 idealnya memiliki struktur:

1. Overview
2. Historical Context
3. Main Events
4. Key Figures
5. Primary Evidence
6. Scholarly Interpretation
7. Institutional Tradition
8. Areas of Disagreement
9. What Is Known
10. What Remains Uncertain
11. Sources
12. Related Objects / People / Places

---

# 16. HANDLING CONTESTED HISTORY

Apabila ada perbedaan:

Jangan:

> “Versi A salah.”

kecuali terdapat dasar evidence yang sangat kuat dan argumen tersebut memang dapat dipertanggungjawabkan.

Lebih baik:

> “Sumber A mencatat X, sementara sumber B mencatat Y. Perbedaan ini belum dapat diselesaikan secara definitif berdasarkan sumber yang tersedia.”

Jika salah satu interpretasi mempunyai evidentiary support yang lebih kuat, jelaskan basisnya dan atribusikan kepada sumber/penelitian terkait.

---

# 17. GENEALOGY & SILSILAH

Untuk silsilah TQN, editorial policy harus sangat ketat.

Setiap node seharusnya memiliki:

- nama,
- variasi nama,
- gelar,
- periode hidup jika diketahui,
- hubungan transmisi,
- sumber hubungan,
- tingkat kepastian,
- catatan perbedaan versi.

Jangan mengubah grafik silsilah menjadi klaim absolut jika sumbernya berbeda.

Silsilah harus dipresentasikan sebagai:

> **documented transmission network with source provenance**

bila bukti memungkinkan.

---

# 18. SACRED / RELIGIOUS CONTENT

Untuk materi terkait tasawuf/TQN:

- gunakan istilah secara hormat,
- jangan menyederhanakan konsep secara menyesatkan,
- bedakan descriptive scholarship dari devotional writing,
- jangan mengklaim otoritas spiritual,
- jangan mengubah konten spiritual menjadi gimmick.

Untuk konteks yang memerlukan otoritas keagamaan, gunakan reviewer yang kompeten.

---

# 19. SPIRITUAL AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

165 tidak boleh membuat konten yang menyiratkan bahwa platform:

- memberikan talqin,
- memberi bai'at,
- mengangkat mursyid,
- memberikan ijazah spiritual,
- menentukan maqam seseorang,
- menentukan siapa yang memiliki legitimasi spiritual berdasarkan keputusan editorial 165.

165 dapat:

- mendokumentasikan sejarah,
- menjelaskan konsep,
- menyimpan sumber,
- mengarsipkan dokumen,
- mendokumentasikan pernyataan institusi,
- menyediakan bahan pendidikan.

# 21. QUOTES & ATTRIBUTION

Kutipan tokoh sangat sensitif.

Sebelum menerbitkan:

- cari sumber asli,
- verifikasi wording,
- verifikasi bahasa,
- verifikasi konteks,
- verifikasi atribusi,
- cari sumber kedua bila tersedia.

Jika sumber asli tidak ditemukan, jangan menulis:

> “Syekh X berkata...”

Gunakan formulasi yang lebih jujur seperti:

> “Kutipan ini beredar dalam sumber sekunder, tetapi sumber primer belum ditemukan oleh 165.”

---

# 22. MIRACLES / KARĀMĀT / DEVOTIONAL NARRATIVES

165 dapat mendokumentasikan bahwa suatu riwayat hidup dalam tradisi.

Namun jangan mengubah tradisi menjadi klaim ilmiah tanpa dasar.

Contoh framing:

> “Dalam tradisi/manāqib yang beredar di lingkungan X, diriwayatkan bahwa...”

bukan:

> “Telah terbukti bahwa...”

---

# 23. MANUSCRIPT EDITORIAL POLICY

Untuk manuskrip:

- jangan menyimpulkan authenticity tanpa examination yang memadai,
- simpan image/master preservation file,
- simpan transcription,
- simpan transliteration bila diperlukan,
- catat editorial intervention,
- catat damaged/missing sections,
- bedakan original text dengan reconstruction.

---

# 24. OCR POLICY

OCR hanya menghasilkan:

> **machine-generated transcription candidate**

Bukan automatically authoritative text.

Workflow:

```text
SCAN
  ↓
OCR
  ↓
Human Verification
  ↓
Correction
  ↓
Versioned Transcript
  ↓
Publication
```

Untuk aksara atau manuskrip sulit dibaca, confidence per passage dapat digunakan.

---

# 25. TRANSLITERATION

165 perlu memiliki standard transliteration.

Standard harus mencakup:

- Arabic,
- Jawi/Pegon bila relevan,
- Indonesian names,
- Persian/Turkish variants bila relevan,
- historical orthography.

Retain original spelling where historically significant.

Search system harus tetap mengenali:

- transliteration variants,
- alternative spellings,
- titles,
- aliases.

---

# 26. TRANSLATION

Terjemahan harus dibedakan dari teks asli.

Untuk istilah teknis:

- pertahankan original term,
- berikan transliteration,
- berikan explanatory translation,
- jangan memaksakan satu kata Inggris jika kehilangan makna.

Contoh pola:

> **Mahabbah (محبة)** — love/devotional love; dalam konteks tertentu memiliki makna spiritual yang lebih khusus.

---

# 27. MULTILINGUAL EDITORIAL RULE

Bahasa target tidak boleh menghasilkan klaim yang lebih pasti daripada bahasa sumber.

Jika bahasa Indonesia mengatakan:

> “Menurut sumber A...”

versi Inggris tidak boleh berubah menjadi:

> “It is established that...”

Translation must preserve:

- uncertainty,
- attribution,
- chronology,
- names,
- titles,
- quotations,
- caveats.

---

# 28. PHOTOGRAPHY

Setiap historical photograph sebaiknya mencatat:

- object ID,
- date or estimated date,
- location,
- people,
- photographer if known,
- source/owner,
- provenance,
- original caption,
- editorial caption,
- rights.

Do not identify people solely from visual guesswork when identity is uncertain.

Use:

> “believed to be”

atau

> “possibly”

dengan supporting evidence.

---

# 29. AUDIO & VIDEO

Editorial package sebaiknya mencakup:

- recording date,
- speaker(s),
- location,
- recorder if known,
- source,
- consent status,
- transcript,
- timestamps,
- translation if available,
- editing notes.

Do not silently remove passages in a way that changes the historical meaning.

---

# 30. ORAL HISTORY

Oral history adalah evidence penting tetapi memiliki karakteristik berbeda dari documentary records.

Interview documentation harus mencatat:

- interviewee,
- interviewer,
- date,
- location,
- consent,
- relationship to topic,
- relevant background,
- recording,
- transcript,
- editorial notes.

Oral testimony should not automatically be converted into objective fact without corroboration where corroboration is appropriate.

---

# 31. SOCIAL MEDIA EDITORIAL RULE

Social posts are derivative content.

Core rule:

> **Short format may simplify; it must not falsify.**

Setiap short-form content harus mempertahankan:

- attribution,
- essential context,
- uncertainty when material,
- source trail.

Clickbait that materially distorts the underlying research is prohibited.

---

# 32. HEADLINES

Headline harus:

- menarik,
- informatif,
- tidak menyesatkan,
- tidak melebih-lebihkan evidence.

Hindari:

> “RAHASIA TERBESAR TQN AKHIRNYA TERBONGKAR!”

kecuali substansi benar-benar mendukung klaim tersebut, dan dalam praktik editorial 165 gaya seperti ini sebaiknya tidak menjadi default.

Utamakan:

> “Bagaimana TQN Berkembang di Jawa?”

---

# 33. SEO RULE

SEO tidak boleh mengalahkan accuracy.

Jangan:

- keyword stuffing,
- misleading title,
- fake authority,
- fabricated expert quotes,
- doorway pages,
- duplicated low-value content.

SEO objective:

> **make trustworthy knowledge discoverable.**

---

# 34. SENSITIVE CLAIMS

Extra review dibutuhkan untuk:

- accusations,
- allegations,
- disputed succession,
- institutional conflict,
- authenticity claims,
- financial allegations,
- living persons,
- legal disputes,
- private information,
- sensitive family history.

Do not publish private information merely because it is interesting.

---

# 35. LIVING PERSONS

Untuk orang yang masih hidup:

- verify names,
- avoid unnecessary personal details,
- seek consent where appropriate,
- distinguish fact from allegation,
- provide attribution.

Do not publish rumors as reporting.

---

# 36. DEFAMATION / REPUTATIONAL RISK

Before publishing potentially damaging claims, ask:

1. Is it materially relevant?
2. Is there evidence?
3. Is the attribution clear?
4. Is the wording proportionate?
5. Has the person/context been represented fairly?
6. Is publication necessary?
7. Is there a less harmful but accurate way to state the fact?

Escalate high-risk material for legal review where appropriate.

---

# 37. CORRECTIONS

165 must prefer correction over concealment.

Correction levels:

### TYPO
Minor spelling/formatting.

### CLARIFICATION
Meaning improved without changing core fact.

### FACTUAL CORRECTION
A factual statement was materially wrong.

### SUBSTANTIAL REVISION
New evidence materially changes interpretation.

### RETRACTION
Article or claim should no longer stand in published form.

Record:

- date,
- what changed,
- why,
- evidence,
- responsible reviewer.

---

# 38. RETRACTION

Retraction should be considered when:

- source was fabricated,
- material attribution was false,
- evidence was materially misrepresented,
- major factual error cannot be repaired without invalidating the article,
- legal or ethical grounds require removal.

Do not delete history silently.

Where feasible, preserve an archival record that the article existed and was later retracted, without retaining sensitive material that should not remain public.

---

# 39. VERSIONING

Every important publication should have:

- publication date,
- updated date,
- version,
- change note.

Historical content should preserve its editorial history.

---

# 40. COMMENTS & USER CONTRIBUTIONS

User submissions are not automatically verified.

Community contributions should have status:

`SUBMITTED → TRIAGED → REVIEW → VERIFIED / PARTIALLY VERIFIED / UNVERIFIED / REJECTED`

User-generated claims should not appear visually equivalent to verified archival evidence unless status is visible.

---

# 41. CORRECTION SUBMISSIONS

165 should provide a mechanism for:

> **Suggest a correction**

A correction submission should permit:

- claim,
- reason,
- evidence,
- source,
- submitter contact where optional/appropriate.

No correction is accepted solely because the submitter is senior, famous, or influential.

---

# 42. EDITORIAL CONFLICTS

Editorial staff must not suppress evidence simply because it is inconvenient to:

- founder,
- donors,
- sponsors,
- institutions,
- partners,
- community members,
- commercial interests.

The response to inconvenient evidence is:

> investigate, contextualize, and attribute.

---

# 43. SPONSORSHIP

Sponsor funding cannot purchase historical conclusions.

Sponsored material must clearly identify:

- sponsor,
- nature of relationship,
- whether sponsor had editorial control.

Editorial research must remain independent.

---

# 44. COMMERCIAL CONTENT

Three categories:

### Editorial
Produced under editorial standards.

### Sponsored
Financial relationship exists and is disclosed.

### Promotional
Designed primarily to promote a product/service/event.

Do not blur these categories.

---

# 45. AI POLICY

AI may assist with:

- brainstorming,
- summarization,
- translation drafts,
- transcription assistance,
- metadata suggestions,
- classification,
- OCR post-processing,
- content repurposing,
- search,
- entity matching,
- quality-control assistance.

AI must not independently establish high-risk historical claims.

For important outputs:

```text
AI ASSIST
   ↓
SOURCE CHECK
   ↓
HUMAN REVIEW
   ↓
EDITORIAL APPROVAL
   ↓
PUBLICATION
```

---

# 46. AI HALLUCINATION CONTROL

When AI proposes a historical statement:

1. identify every factual claim,
2. locate source,
3. verify source content,
4. verify attribution,
5. verify chronology,
6. verify wording,
7. remove unsupported details.

A fluent sentence is not evidence.

---

# 47. AI DISCLOSURE

165 may disclose AI assistance when relevant to transparency.

For archival transcription or translation, retain internal records of:

- model/tool,
- date,
- prompt/workflow when appropriate,
- human reviewer,
- revision.

This creates an audit trail.

# 49. RISK-BASED REVIEW

Not every content item needs the same review level.

## LOW RISK

- routine formatting,
- non-sensitive event notice,
- simple media derivative.

## MEDIUM RISK

- historical interpretation,
- biography,
- institutional history,
- translation of scholarly material.

## HIGH RISK

- disputed history,
- living person allegations,
- sensitive genealogy,
- manuscript authenticity,
- spiritual authority claims,
- legal issues,
- high-impact institutional disputes.

High-risk content requires additional review.

- clear,
- scholarly when necessary,
- accessible to non-specialists,
- spiritually respectful,
- historically disciplined,
- internationally understandable.

Avoid:

- needless sensationalism,
- superiority claims,
- sectarian provocation,
- unverified miracle claims as fact,
- excessive jargon,
- clickbait.

---

# 55. AUDIENCE LAYERS

165 should publish at three levels:

### LEVEL 1 — PUBLIC

Simple language.

### LEVEL 2 — EDUCATIONAL

More detailed context, timeline, references.

### LEVEL 3 — RESEARCH

Full citations, primary sources, notes, metadata, transcription, archival records.

One source base can support all three layers.

# 58. EDITORIAL INDEPENDENCE FROM SOCIAL ALGORITHMS

Editorial decisions must not be determined solely by:

- views,
- likes,
- shares,
- watch time,
- follower growth.

Metrics can inform distribution.

They must not determine historical truth.

---

# 59. CONTENT REUSE

Reuse is encouraged when it preserves context.

One verified source can become:

- long-form article,
- short-form educational post,
- infographic,
- podcast segment,
- documentary chapter,
- newsletter,
- glossary entry.

However, the shortened version cannot create a false impression absent from the original.

---

# 60. ATTRIBUTION OF TRADITION

When documenting a tradition, identify:

- who preserves the tradition,
- where it is transmitted,
- what kind of source carries it,
- whether independent corroboration exists.

Respect does not require pretending that all traditions are documentary facts.

---

# 61. EDITORIAL USE OF “OFFICIAL”

The term **official** must be used narrowly.

Use “official” only when 165 has a legitimate basis to describe an institution, document, statement, or relationship as official.

Avoid using “official” as a branding shortcut for authority not formally granted.

---

# 62. CLAIMS ABOUT LEGITIMACY

165 must not independently declare a person or institution:

- spiritually legitimate,
- illegitimate,
- the “true” lineage,
- the sole representative,
- the sole authority,

unless merely reporting a clearly attributable claim.

Where competing claims exist, document the positions and evidence.

---

# 63. RELIGIOUS DIVERSITY & INTERNAL DIFFERENCES

Differences between TQN communities may concern:

- terminology,
- organizational history,
- transmission accounts,
- practices,
- institutional interpretation,
- historical chronology.

165 should document differences without unnecessarily inflaming them.

---

# 64. INTERNATIONAL EDITORIAL STANDARD

Global audiences may not understand Indonesian religious terminology.

Every major introductory page should answer:

- What is this?
- Where does it come from?
- When did it develop?
- Who are the important figures?
- What sources support it?
- What remains uncertain?
- What terminology should readers know?

---

# 65. ACCESSIBILITY

Editorial content should be understandable through:

- headings,
- short paragraphs,
- descriptive link text,
- alt text,
- captions,
- transcripts,
- readable contrast,
- keyboard-accessible interfaces where implemented.

Accessibility applies to archive and multimedia content as well as articles.

---

# 66. PRIVACY

Do not publish personal information merely because it exists in an archive.

Before publication consider:

- consent,
- sensitivity,
- public interest,
- historical necessity,
- risk to living people,
- legal requirements.

Private material may require restricted access.

---

# 67. CHILDREN / VULNERABLE SUBJECTS

Extra care is required when documenting:

- minors,
- victims,
- vulnerable people,
- sensitive family histories,
- medical information,
- traumatic events.

Use the minimum necessary information.

---

# 68. EDITORIAL ARCHIVE

165 must preserve:

- drafts of significant works,
- source packets,
- reviewer notes where appropriate,
- previous versions,
- correction records,
- withdrawn/retracted material,
- metadata,
- publication dates.

The editorial history is itself institutional memory.

---

# 69. EDITORIAL AUDIT

At least periodically, audit:

- factual errors,
- outdated claims,
- broken sources,
- dead links,
- terminology inconsistencies,
- translation drift,
- rights status,
- AI-generated content,
- misleading headlines.

Audit results should feed back into Document 000 and the correction/change system.

---

# 70. CORRECTION CULTURE

165 should treat corrections as evidence of institutional maturity.

Guiding principle:

> **Being corrected is less damaging than being known to hide errors.**

Corrections should be factual, calm, timestamped, and non-defensive.

---

# 71. EDITORIAL ETHOS

The editorial culture should reward:

- careful research,
- transparent uncertainty,
- source discovery,
- correction,
- respectful disagreement,
- primary-source work,
- preservation,
- intellectual humility.

It should not reward:

- sensationalism,
- fabricated certainty,
- citation theater,
- authority by volume,
- outrage farming,
- engagement at any cost.

# 74. RED FLAGS THAT BLOCK PUBLICATION

Publication should pause when:

- a primary quotation cannot be verified,
- a citation does not support the claim,
- an important date is invented,
- a photograph's identity is asserted without basis,
- a disputed lineage is presented as uncontested,
- rights are unknown for a sensitive asset,
- AI generated unsupported facts,
- a living person's reputation may be harmed without sufficient evidence,
- a sponsor is attempting to control editorial conclusion.

Founder / Executive / Governing Body according to Document 007.

# 78. EDITORIAL GOVERNANCE RULE

No person has the right to override evidence merely through status, title, wealth, popularity, or proximity to the founder.

Likewise, no editor should manufacture authority merely through academic language.

Evidence and attribution remain primary.

---

# 79. FOUNDER RELATIONSHIP

Founder may establish strategic direction and mission but must not require editorial staff to falsify, suppress, or misrepresent historical evidence.

Founder-related material is subject to the same source standards as other historical subjects.

The founder's status does not itself convert a claim into historical fact.

---

# 80. BUSINESS RELATIONSHIP

Revenue objectives are legitimate institutional objectives.

However:

> **Revenue does not determine historical truth.**

Commercial products can prioritize packaging, convenience, design, access, service, and professional utility.

They cannot manufacture evidence.

---

# 81. PUBLIC CORRECTION CHANNEL

165 should eventually provide a clear public interface:

> **Report a factual error**

Minimum inputs:

- page/content,
- statement in question,
- proposed correction,
- evidence/source,
- optional contact.

All serious submissions should enter a correction workflow.
