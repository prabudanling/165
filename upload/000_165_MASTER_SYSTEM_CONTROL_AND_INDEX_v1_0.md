# 3. TRUTH & SOURCE MODEL

Setiap knowledge object harus dapat dibedakan menurut status epistemiknya.

## TIER A — PRIMARY / DOCUMENTARY EVIDENCE

Contoh:

- manuscripts,
- letters,
- official institutional documents,
- dated photographs,
- archival records,
- recordings,
- contemporaneous publications,
- original inscriptions.

## TIER B — SCHOLARLY / SECONDARY SOURCES

Contoh:

- peer-reviewed research,
- academic books,
- theses/dissertations,
- critical editions,
- reputable reference works.

## TIER C — INSTITUTIONAL / LIVING TRADITION

Contoh:

- institutional histories,
- manaqib,
- family records,
- oral histories,
- teacher/student testimony,
- community traditions.

## TIER D — 165 EDITORIAL SYNTHESIS

Interpretasi, synthesis, educational framing, timeline construction, or explanatory models prepared by 165.

**Rule:** TIER D tidak boleh dipresentasikan seolah-olah merupakan primary evidence.

# 16. RESEARCH GOVERNANCE

Every research-heavy publication should identify:

- question,
- scope,
- sources,
- methodology,
- evidence,
- uncertainty,
- attribution,
- competing interpretations where relevant,
- editorial conclusion,
- revision history.

> **Document the disagreement instead of hiding it.**
